Back-to-Back Long Runs

Practice tired legs without wrecking your week.

Updated September 8, 2026

Why back-to-backs show up in ultra plans

Ultras ask you to keep moving on tired legs. A Saturday medium-long + Sunday long (or the reverse) teaches that without a single monster day that blows up your Monday meeting. Use them inside the 16-week plan when life allows — not every weekend.

Simple rules

  • Both days easy effort — ego stays home
  • Day 1 is shorter; Day 2 is the main long run
  • Practice fueling on Day 2 especially
  • Skip the double if sleep, kids, or a niggle are already loud
  • Follow with easier midweek, not a hammer session

Busy-life version: One quality back-to-back every 2–3 weeks beats forcing it weekly and showing up wrecked at work.
